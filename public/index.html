    How Nadir picks companies

    A strong record

    Nadir looks at the three years before this week: how fast the share price grew, how steady that climb was, and how close it was to its high.

    A real stumble

    Then it measures this week's fall, both in size and against how much the stock normally moves in a week. A 6% drop means more for a calm company than for a jumpy one.

    One score

    The score needs both. A great company that barely moved scores low, and so does a big drop in a company without a track record.
